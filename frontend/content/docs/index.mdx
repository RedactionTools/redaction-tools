This catalog exists because redaction software is bought under pressure — a disclosure
deadline, an audit, a breach — and the market is hard to compare. Vendors publish prices
in different units, some publish none at all, and "free" can mean a free tier or a
fortnight's trial depending on who wrote the page.

These pages say how we deal with that: what we check, what we refuse to do, and how to
tell us we are wrong.

## Start here

**[Methodology](/docs/methodology)** is the one to read first. It covers where every
published figure comes from, how provenance is marked, what a tool has to clear before it
becomes a page, and how corrections work. If you only read one page, read that one.

## For buyers

[Reading a listing](/docs/buying/reading-a-listing) walks through every field on a tool's
page and says what it does and does not claim. [Pricing units](/docs/buying/pricing-units)
explains why two published prices usually cannot be compared as printed, and
[the price calculator](/docs/buying/price-calculator) is the answer to that — it costs each
plan against a volume you supply, using the vendor's own rate. The
[glossary](/docs/buying/glossary) defines the terms the filters use.

## For vendors

If your tool is listed, or should be: [submitting a tool](/docs/vendors/submitting-a-tool),
[claiming a listing](/docs/vendors/claiming-a-listing),
[correcting a price](/docs/vendors/correcting-a-price),
[what you can change](/docs/vendors/what-you-can-change) and
[logos and screenshots](/docs/vendors/logos-and-screenshots).

The short version: you are the authority on what you charge, and no vendor has a path to a
published number that does not pass a person.

## Developers

The catalog is readable as JSON and as plain text. See
[the REST API](/docs/developers/rest-api) and [the MCP server](/docs/developers/mcp-server).

Machine-readable summaries live at [`/llms.txt`](/llms.txt) and
[`/llms-full.txt`](/llms-full.txt).
