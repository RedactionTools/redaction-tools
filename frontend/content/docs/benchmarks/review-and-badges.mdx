## Who published it

Every result names the account that published it, and in what role. The role is recorded
when the result is sent and is not updated later:

| Role                | Meaning                                                                           |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Redaction Tools** | Our own staff                                                                     |
| **Tool owner**      | An account with an approved [claim](/docs/vendors/claiming-a-listing) on the tool |
| **Community**       | Anyone else                                                                       |

An account without a name is shown as _Anonymous contributor_, never by its email address.

## Review

Nothing reaches a leaderboard until an editor has approved it, and a submission is approved
or rejected as a whole. Our own staff's results are the only exception: they are published
once scored. A rejection always comes with a note, which you can read on
[your submissions](/benchmarks/submissions). You can withdraw a submission at any point
before it is approved.

## How far a number can be trusted

| Badge                       | Meaning                                                     |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Scored by us**            | The files were uploaded and our scorer produced the numbers |
| **Self-scored · verified**  | Scored with the CLI; our rescore agreed                     |
| **Self-scored**             | Scored with the CLI; we could not rescore it                |
| **Disputed by our rescore** | Scored with the CLI; our rescore disagreed                  |

A leaderboard row takes the badge of its least trustworthy run.
