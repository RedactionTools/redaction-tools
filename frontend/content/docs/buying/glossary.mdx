Every filter on the catalog is built from one of eight dimensions. Filtering is **OR within
a dimension and AND across them**: picking PDF and Image widens the result, then adding
Self-hosted narrows it.

A tool must have a value recorded on media, deployment and method before it can be listed
at all. The other five are optional and often sparse.

## Redaction, and what it is not

**Redaction** is the permanent removal of information from a document, such that it cannot
be recovered from the file.

**Masking** covers information up. A black rectangle drawn over a paragraph in a PDF
viewer hides it from a reader and leaves the text in the file, where anyone can select,
copy or extract it. This is the single most common way a "redacted" document leaks, and it
is why **true content removal** is tracked as its own capability below.

## Media

What the tool can redact.

- **PDF** — the default case: documents with a text layer, and scans without one.
- **Image** — JPEG, PNG and similar, where there is no text layer to remove.
- **Video** — moving footage, usually faces, licence plates and screens.
- **Audio** — spoken content, typically bleeped or silenced.
- **Text and logs** — plain text, CSV, JSON, application logs.

## Deployment

Where the tool runs, which is usually the first question a regulated buyer asks.

- **Online** — a hosted web application. Your documents go to the vendor.
- **Desktop** — installed software; files stay on the machine.
- **Self-hosted** — you run it on your own infrastructure.
- **API** — programmatic access, for redaction inside your own pipeline.
- **Browser extension** — runs inside the browser against what is on screen.

## Redaction method

How the tool decides what to cover.

- **Manual** — a person selects each region. Slow and fully accountable.
- **Rule-based** — patterns and dictionaries: card numbers, national ID formats, a list of
  names.
- **AI-powered** — a model identifies entities, faces or speech.
- **Hybrid** — automated detection with a human review step. The common shape for
  high-stakes work.

## Platform

Operating systems and runtimes: Windows · macOS · Linux · iOS · Android · Web

## Capability

Specific features, and the dimension worth reading closely.

- **True content removal** — the underlying data is destroyed, not covered. See above.
- **OCR** — reads text out of scans, without which a scanned PDF cannot be searched for
  anything to redact.
- **Batch processing** — many files in one operation.
- **Metadata removal** — strips author, revision history and EXIF, which survive a visual
  redaction untouched.
- **Audit log** — a record of who redacted what and when.
- **Face detection**, **Licence plate detection** — automatic detection in images and
  video.
- **Entity detection** — names, addresses, account numbers and similar in text.
- **Speech to text** — transcription, a prerequisite for redacting audio by content.
- **Webhooks**, **SSO**, **Air-gapped** — integration and deployment constraints.

## Audience

Who the vendor sells to: Legal · Healthcare · Government · Law enforcement, among others.
A tool filed under an audience is one whose own marketing targets it, not one we have
judged suitable for it.
